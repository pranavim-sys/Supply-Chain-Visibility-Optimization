# Milestone 4: Supply Chain Visibility & Optimization

This milestone focuses on developing the Warehouse Efficiency Dashboard and the Executive Dashboard in Power BI.

**Warehouse Efficiency Dashboard**

Focuses on operational performance inside the warehouse.

Tracks metrics like warehouse utilization, throughput, labor productivity, order accuracy, and cycle times.

Helps managers identify bottlenecks, monitor daily operations, and improve efficiency at the ground level.

It’s more detailed and tactical, designed for supervisors and operations teams.

**Executive Dashboard**

Provides a high-level view for leadership.

Summarizes critical KPIs such as overall supply chain costs, service levels, forecast accuracy, and efficiency trends.

Uses simple, visual layouts (gauges, charts, scorecards) to highlight exceptions and strategic insights.

Helps executives make quick, data-driven decisions without diving into operational details.

# Warehouse utilization

Warehouse utilization calculation is a way to measure how efficiently the available storage space in a warehouse is being used.

It is calculated by comparing the amount of space currently occupied (such as pallets, bins, or racks filled with goods) against the total available storage capacity.

If utilization is too low, it means space is underused and resources are wasted.

If utilization is too high (above ~85%), it can cause congestion, reduce efficiency, and make it harder to handle peak demand.

The ideal range is usually 70–85%, balancing efficiency with flexibility for incoming goods.

# Throughput and productivity KPIs

Throughput and productivity KPIs are used to measure how efficiently a warehouse or operation is performing.

Throughput: This shows how many units (orders, pallets, cartons, etc.) are processed in a given time period.

Productivity: This measures how effectively labor is being used, usually expressed as units processed per labor hour.

Throughput = speed of work (how much gets done in a time frame).

Productivity = efficiency of work (how much each worker contributes).

# An executive dashboard design methodology

An executive dashboard design methodology is about creating a clear, high-level view of business performance that helps leaders make quick, informed decisions.

Purpose: To give executives a snapshot of critical KPIs (like warehouse utilization, throughput, costs, and forecasts) in one place.

Principles: Keep it simple, role-based, and focused on actionable insights rather than raw data.

Design Steps:

Identify the most important KPIs for decision-making.

Use intuitive visuals (gauges, charts, heatmaps) to highlight trends and exceptions.

Provide drill-down options for deeper analysis when needed.

Ensure consistency in layout, colors, and data sources.

A forecasting implementation approach

A forecasting implementation approach is the structured way of predicting future warehouse or business performance using historical data and analytical models.

Purpose: To anticipate demand, labor needs, and space utilization so operations can be planned proactively.

Steps:

Collect and clean historical data (orders, shipments, labor hours, utilization).

Choose forecasting techniques (time-series models like ARIMA, exponential smoothing, or machine learning models).

Train and validate models to ensure accuracy.

Deploy forecasts into dashboards for real-time monitoring.

Outputs: Demand forecasts, labor requirement forecasts, and warehouse space utilization forecasts.

Benefit: Helps reduce costs, avoid bottlenecks, and improve service levels by preparing for future demand.

# Dashboard optimization techniques

Dashboard optimization techniques are methods used to make dashboards faster, easier to use, and more effective for decision-making.

Performance Optimization: Improve speed by using aggregated tables, incremental refresh, and efficient queries so dashboards load quickly.

Design Optimization: Keep visuals simple, avoid clutter, and use consistent colors and layouts to highlight key KPIs clearly.

User Experience: Add interactive filters (like date, product, region), ensure mobile-friendly design, and tailor views for different roles.

Security & Access: Provide role-based access so each user sees only relevant data.

# Key insights and business recommendations

Key insights and business recommendations are the actionable takeaways derived from analyzing warehouse data and KPIs.

**Key Insights:**

High warehouse utilization (above ~85%) can cause congestion and slow operations.

Low productivity indicates inefficiencies in labor or processes.

Forecast accuracy directly impacts inventory costs and service levels.

Balanced throughput ensures smooth order fulfillment without overloading staff.

**Business Recommendations:**

Maintain utilization between 70–85% for efficiency and flexibility.

Invest in automation and training to boost productivity.

Regularly monitor KPIs to identify bottlenecks early.

Use predictive analytics to plan for peak demand and reduce costs.
